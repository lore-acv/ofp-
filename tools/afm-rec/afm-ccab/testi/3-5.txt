819 239-545 | proceeding as follows:
891 306-817 | (1) Perform “before landing” check.
927 306-1349 | (2) Drag over selected field with flaps 20° and 121 km/h - 65 kts - 75 MPH
964 305-1395 | airspeed, noting the preferred area for touchdown for the next landing approach.
1001 305-978 | Then retract flaps after well clear of all obstacles.
1037 306-1320 | (3) On downwind leg, turn off all switches except the ignition and master
1075 306-436 | switches.
1109 305-1152 | (4) Approach with flaps 40° at 121 km/h - 65 kts - 75 MPH.
1146 305-972 | (5) Unlatch cabin doors prior to final approach.
1182 306-1203 | (6) Before touchdown, turn ignition and master switches “OFF.”
1219 306-848 | (7) Land in a slightly tail-low attitude.

1471 239-1400 | 85 MPH. If time permits, attempt to restart the engine by checking for fuel quantity,
