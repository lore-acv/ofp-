1499 titolo 181-782 | FLIGHT IN ICING CONDITIONS.
1569 244-1284 | Although flying in known icing conditions is prohibited, an unexpected icing
1606 178-727 | encounter should be handled as follows:
1677 245-894 | (1) Turn pitot heat switch “ON” (if installed).
1713 244-1300 | (2) Turn back or change altitude to obtain an outside air temperature that is
1748 244-564 | less conducive to icing.
1783 244-1330 | (3) Pull cabin heat control full out to obtain windshield defroster airflow. Ad-
1819 241-1129 | just cabin air control to get maximum defroster heat and airflow.
1853 244-1112 | (4) Increase RPM to minimize ice build-up on propeller blades.
1888 244-1330 | (5) Plan a landing at the nearest airport. With an extremely rapid ice build-up,
1924 244-819 | select a suitable “off airport” landing site.
1958 242-1279 | (6) With an ice accumulation of 0,5 cm or more on the wing leading edges,
1993 242-878 | be prepared for significantly higher stall speed.
2029 243-1285 | (7) Leave wing flaps retracted. With a severe ice build-up on the horizontal
2063 243-1288 | tail, the change in wing wake airflow direction caused by wing flap extension
2099 243-868 | could result in a loss of elevator effectiveness.
