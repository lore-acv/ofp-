613 182-688 | AUXILIARY FUEL PUMP SWITCH.
686 248-1321 | The down position of the auxiliary fuel pump switch, labeled “LOW,” operates
722 182-1345 | the pump at low speed. With the switch in this position, the pump supplies sufficient
758 182-1222 | fuel for priming and starting the engine.
835 247-1327 | The up position of the switch, labeled “HIGH,” is used for engine operation if the
868 182-1342 | engine-driven pump should fail, or for vapor purging in extremely hot weather. When
905 181-1321 | the switch is in this position, the pump operates at one of two flow rates depending
941 181-1346 | upon the setting of the throttle. With the throttle at a cruise setting, the pump is op-
978 181-1336 | erating at maximum capacity, supplying sufficient fuel flow to maintain flight. When
1013 181-1303 | the throttle is moved toward the closed position, the auxiliary fuel pump flow rate
1050 181-1326 | is automatically reduced, preventing an excessively rich mixture during periods of re-
1087 181-460 | duced engine speed.
1162 247-1284 | The auxiliary fuel pump is not to be used while the engine is running during
1204 180-1313 | normal operation, because, with the engine-driven pump functioning, a fuel/air ratio
1239 181-1286 | considerably richer than best power is produced. However, for vapor elimination,
1275 181-1303 | the fuel pump may be used with the engine running and the switch in the “HIGH”
1311 180-1333 | position. The mixture must then be leaned as required to prevent an excessively rich
1348 180-306 | mixture.
1419 247-1328 | To ensure a prompt engine restart in flight after running a fuel tank dry, switch
1456 181-1318 | to the tank containing fuel and place the auxiliary fuel pump switch in the “HIGH”
1492 180-1183 | position momentarily (3 to 5 seconds) with the throttle at least 1/2 open.
1565 246-1335 | If the propeller should stop (possible at very low airspeeds) before the tank con-
1601 179-1320 | taining fuel is selected, place the auxiliary fuel pump switch in the “HIGH” position
1635 180-1271 | and advance the throttle promptly until the fuel flow indicator registers approxi-
1670 180-1265 | mately 1/2 way into the green arc for 1 to 2 seconds duration. Then retard the
1707 180-1330 | throttle, turn off the auxiliary fuel pump, and use the starter to turn the engine over
1743 178-513 | until a start is obtained.
