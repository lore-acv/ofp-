906 titolo | MOORING YOUR AIRPLANE.
967 | Proper tie-down procedure is your best precaution against damage to your park-
998 | ed airplane by gusty or strong winds. To tie-down your airplane securely, proceed
1028 | as follows:
1090 | (1) Set the parking brake and install the control wheel lock.
1121 | (2) Tie sufficiently strong ropes or chains (300 kg tensile strength) to wing,
1152 | tail, and nose tie-down fittings and secure each rope to a ramp tie-down.
1182 | (3) Install a surface control lock over the fin and rudder.
1213 | (4) Install a pitot tube cover.
1305 titolo | WINDSHIELD-WINDOWS.
1364 | The plastic windshield and windows should be cleaned with an aircraft wind-
1395 | shield cleaner. Apply the cleaner sparingly with soft cloths, and rub with moderate
1425 | pressure until all dirt, oil scum and bug stains are removed. Allow the cleaner to dry,
1455 | then wipe it off with soft flannel cloths.
1516 | If a windshield cleaner is not available, the plastic can be cleaned with soft cloths
1548 | moistened with Stoddard solvent to remove oil and grease.
