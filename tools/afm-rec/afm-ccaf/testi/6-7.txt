329 titolo | OPTIONAL SYSTEMS
434 titolo 380-900 c=29 | AUXILIARY FUEL TANK SYSTEM
525 | An optional auxiliary fuel tank system (figure 6-1) is available to increase the
556 | airplane operating range. System components include a 17 U.S. gallon (64 litres)
586 | fuel tank (17 U.S. gallons -64 litres usable) installed on the baggage compartment
617 | floor, an electric fuel transfer pump behind the tank, an electrically-operated fuel
648 | quantity indicator and fuel transfer pump switch on the instrument panel, a fuel
679 | tank filler provision on the right side of the fuselage, a fuel tank sump drain valve
709 | at the front of the tank on the bottom of the fuselage, and the necessary plumbing.
771 | The auxiliary fuel system is connected to the right main fuel tank plumbing
802 | above the right cabin door.
898 titolo | AUXILIARY FUEL SYSTEM OPERATION.
957 | To operate the auxiliary fuel system, proceed as follows:
1020 | PRE-FLIGHT CHECK.
1082 | (1) Turn on master switch and check auxiliary tank fuel quantity indicator
1112 | for reading.
1144 | (2) Momentarily pull on transfer pump switch and listen for pump operation.
1175 | Turn off master switch.
1205 | (3) Check quantity of fuel in tank for agreement with fuel quantity indicator.
1237 | Fill tank for anticipated requirements.
1267 | (4) Drain small amount of fuel from fuel tank drain valve to check for pos-
1298 | sible water and sediment.
1357 | DURING FLIGHT.
1418 | (1) Take-off, climb and land with fuel selector valve handle set on “BOTH”
1447 | for maximum safety.
1477 | (2) After leveling off at cruise altitude, switch to “RIGHT” and operate from
1507 | this tank until the fuel supply is exhausted.
1540 | (3) Switch to “LEFT” for operation, then pull on transfer pump switch and
1571 | refill right main fuel tank from auxiliary tank. Push transfer pump switch off
1601 | when fuel transfer is completed.
