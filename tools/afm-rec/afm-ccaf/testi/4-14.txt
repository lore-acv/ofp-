620 | BALKED LANDING.
686 | In a balked landing (go-around) climb, the wing flap setting should be reduced
717 | to 20° immediately after full power is applied. This may be approximated by plac-
748 | ing the flap switch in the “UP” position for a 2 second count, and then returning
779 | the switch to neutral. Upon reaching a safe airspeed and an altitude above immedi-
810 | ate obstacles, flaps should be slowly retracted to the full up position.

1026 | STARTING.
1088 | Prior to starting on a cold morning, it is advisable to pull the propeller through
1119 | several times by hand to “break loose” or “limber” the oil, thus conserving battery
1149 | energy.
1212 | NOTE
1273 | When pulling the propeller through by hand, treat it as if the
1302 | ignition switch is turned on. A loose or broken ground wire
1332 | on either magneto could cause the engine to fire.
1391 | In extremely cold (-20°C and lower) weather, the use of an external preheater and
1421 | an external power source are recommended to reduce wear and abuse to the engine
1451 | and the electrical system. When using an external power source, the position of the
1482 | master switch is important. Refer to Section VI, paragraph Ground Service Plug
1512 | Receptacle, for operating details.
