453 titolo | PROPELLER CARE.
515 | Preflight inspection of propeller blades for nicks, and wiping them occasionally
546 | with an oily cloth to clean off grass and bug stains will assure long, trouble-free ser-
577 | vice. Small nicks on the propeller, particularly near the tips and on the leading edges,
607 | should be dressed out as soon as possible since these nicks produce stress concentra-
638 | tions, and if ignored, may result in cracks. Never use an alkaline cleaner on the
667 | blades; remove grease and dirt with carbon tetrachloride or Stoddard solvent.
792 titolo | INTERIOR CARE.
854 | To remove dust and loose dirt from the upholstery and carpet, clean the interior
885 | regularly with a vacuum cleaner.
947 | Blot up any spilled liquid promptly, with cleansing tissue or rags. Don’t pat the
978 | spot; press the blotting material firmly and hold it for several seconds. Continue
1009 | blotting until no more liquid is taken up. Scrape off sticky materials with a dull
1040 | knife, then spot-clean the area.
1101 | Oily spots may be cleaned with household spot removers, used sparingly. Before
1131 | using any solvent, read the instructions on the container and test it on an obscure
1162 | place on the fabric to be cleaned. Never saturate the fabric with a volatile solvent;
1193 | it may damage the padding and backing material.
1255 | Soiled upholstery and carpet may be cleaned with foam-type detergent, used
1284 | according to the manufacturer’s instructions. To minimize wetting the fabric, keep
1315 | the foam as dry as possible and remove it with a vacuum cleaner.
1372 | The plastic trim, headliner, instrument panel and control knobs need only be
1403 | wiped off with a damp cloth. Oil and grease on the control wheel and control knobs
1433 | can be removed with a cloth moistened with Stoddard solvent. Volatile solvents,
1462 | such as mentioned in paragraphs on care of the windshield, must never be used since
1492 | they soften and craze the plastic.
