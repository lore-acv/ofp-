641 titolo | DAILY
691 | FUEL TANK FILLERS:
722 | Service after each flight with 100/130 minimum grade fuel. (100/130 low lead
753 | aviation fuel with a lead content limited to 2 c.c. per gallon is also approved.)
784 | The capacity of each wing tank is 26 U.S. gallons (98,5 litres). (To ensure
814 | maximum fuel capacity when refueling, place the fuel selector valve in either
845 | “LEFT” or “RIGHT” position to prevent cross-feeding),
907 | The capacity of the optional baggage area auxiliary fuel tank is 17 U.S. gallons
936 | (64 litres).
1030 | FUEL TANK SUMP DRAINS:
1061 | Before the first flight of the day and after each refueling, use fuel sampler cup
1091 | stored in the map compartment and drain a small amount of fuel from the
1122 | quick-drain valve in each fuel tank sump (one under each wing).
1213 | FUEL STRAINER:
1244 | Before the first flight of the day and after each refueling, pull out fuel strainer
1274 | drain knob for about four seconds to clear fuel strainer of possible water and
1305 | sediment. Release drain knob, then check that strainer drain is closed. If water
1335 | is observed, the fuel reservoir drain valve should be drained and the fuel selector
1365 | valve drain plug should be removed to check for the presence of water.
1454 | OIL DIPSTICK:
1485 | Check oil level before each flight. Do not operate on less than 7 quarts (6,6
1516 | litres). To minimize loss of oil through breather, fill to 8 quart (7,6 litres)
1549 | level for normal flights of less than 3 hours. For extended flight, fill to 10
1579 | quarts (9,5 litres). If optional oil filter is installed, one additional quart is
1608 | required when the filter element is changed.
