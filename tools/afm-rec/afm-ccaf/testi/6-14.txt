566 | A wing leveler may be installed to augment the lateral stability of the airplane.
597 | The system uses the Turn Coordinator for roll and yaw sensing. Vacuum pressure,
628 | from the engine-driven vacuum pump, is routed from the Turn Coordinator to cylin-
660 | der-piston servo units attached to the aileron control system. As the airplane devi-
691 | ates from a wing level attitude, vacuum pressure in the servo units is increased or
722 | relieved as needed to actuate the ailerons to oppose the deviations.
785 | A separately mounted push-pull control knob, labeled “WING LVLR,” is pro-
817 | vided on the lower center portion of the instrument panel to turn the system on and
848 | off. A “ROLL TRIM” control knob on the Turn Coordinator is used for manual
880 | roll trim control to compensate for asymmetrical loading of fuel and passengers, and
911 | to optimize system performance in climb, cruise and let-down.
