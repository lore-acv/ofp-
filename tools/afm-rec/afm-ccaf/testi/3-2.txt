1129 | Improper starting procedures involving the excessive use of auxiliary fuel pump
1160 | operation can cause engine flooding and subsequent collection of fuel on the parking
1192 | ramp. This is sometimes experienced in difficult starts in cold weather where engine
1223 | preheat service is not available. If this occurs, the airplane should be pushed away
1252 | from the fuel puddle before another engine start is attempted. In the rare event that
1282 | the fuel puddle is ignited by exhaust from the tailpipe, proceed as follows:
1343 | (1) Turn auxiliary fuel pump “OFF.”
1373 | (2) Move mixture control to idle cut-off.
1403 | (3) Release parking brake,
1433 | (4) Obtain fire extinguisher.
1463 | (5) Extinguish fire with fire extinguisher.

1554 | If fire extinguisher is not available, move aircraft away from the
