362 | ENGINE FIRE IN FLIGHT.
424 | Although engine fires are extremely rare in flight, the following steps should be
455 | taken if one is encountered:
517 | (1) Pull mixture control to idle cut-off.
545 | (2) Pull fuel shutoff valve knob full out.
576 | (3) Turn master switch “OFF.”
607 | (4) Establish a 193 km/h - 104 kts - 120 MPH glide.
638 | (5) Close cabin heat control and cabin air control.
669 | (6) Select a field suitable for a forced landing.
700 | (7) If fire is not extinguished, increase glide speed in an attempt to find an
731 | airspeed that will provide an incombustible mixture.
762 | (8) Execute a forced landing as described in paragraph Emergency Landing
793 | Without Engine Power. Do not attempt to restart the engine.
886 | CABIN FIRE.
948 | (1) Master Switch - - ”OFF”.
979 | (2) Cabin Heating and Ventilation Controls - - Closed (to avoid drafts).
1040 | NOTE
1101 | Use a portable extinguisher if available. If the fire cannot be ex-
1131 | tinguished, land the aircraft as soon as possible.
1192 | IMPORTANT
1255 | After discharging an extinguisher within a closed cabin, ventilating
1286 | the cabin is recommended.
1374 | WING FIRE.
1435 | (1) Master Switch - - “OFF”.
1466 | (2) Ventilating Controls - - Closed.
1529 | NOTE
1591 | Perform a sideslip to keep flames away from the fuel tank and
1622 | cabin, and land as soon as possible with flaps retracted.
