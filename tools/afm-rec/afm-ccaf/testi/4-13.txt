1243 | SHORT FIELD LANDINGS.
1305 | For short field landings, make a power-off approach at approximately 117 km/h -
1334 | 63 kts - 73 MPH with flaps 40°, and land on the main wheels first. Immediately
1364 | after touchdown, lower the nose gear to the ground and apply heavy braking as re-
1394 | quired. For maximum brake effectiveness after all three wheels are on the ground,
1425 | retract the flaps, hold full nose up elevator and apply maximum possible brake pres-
1455 | sure without sliding the tires.
