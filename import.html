Flight Data Import

New flight. Choose an ACV standard route or load the ForeFlight NavLog, then the weather briefing: they provide route, waypoints, distances and times, then wind, QNH, temperature, METAR, TAF and NOTAM. Once both have been read, proceed to the flight plan.

Standard Routes ACV

Aeroclub Varese routes, read from the club's ForeFlight NavLogs: choosing one is the same as loading that NavLog. Tap it again to clear it. Times and fuel are recomputed in the flight plan.
or

NavLog ForeFlight

Click or drag the NavLog exported from ForeFlight (PDF or text)
Airports, route and distance are taken from here. Speeds and fuel flows are not: those remain the POH figures, which take precedence.

Weather Briefing

Get the briefing on SkyBrief Search DEP, ARR and ALTN by ICAO, download the PDF, drop it below. skybrief.it
Click or drag the WX & NOTAM briefing PDF
METAR, SPECI, TAF and NOTAM are sorted onto DEP, ARR and ALTN, and from there feed wind, QNH and temperature into the performance calculation. Sorting uses the ICAO codes read from the NavLog: load it first if you can.
